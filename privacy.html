Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

FlickTrainer ("we", "the site") is a free browser-based aim training game. This page explains what data is and isn't collected when you use the site.

How the game works

The aim trainer runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your session stats (hits, misses, reaction times) are calculated locally and saved only to your own browser's localStorage so your best scores and recent history persist between visits on the same device. None of this data is transmitted to, or stored on, any server operated by this site. Clearing your browser storage erases it permanently.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display advertisements served by third-party ad networks, such as Google AdSense. These networks may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or generally at aboutads.info.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate traffic (e.g. page views, approximate location by country, device type). This data is not linked to your in-game stats or localStorage history.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the site owner via the contact link in the footer.
